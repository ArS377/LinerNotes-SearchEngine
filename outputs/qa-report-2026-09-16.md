## Verified fixes

ISSUE-002 (high), f161191 — Make failed discovery searches retryable.
Unchanged form submissions previously did nothing; degraded retrievals were cached for five minutes. Reproduced with Jolene. Now explicit and same-form retries show progress and rerun the query, failed retrievals are not cached as success, and partial research is not mislabeled as missing metadata. Jolene and Green Room recovered in Chrome. Four regression tests added across Node/Vitest.

ISSUE-001 (medium), c17fc98 — Keep discovery lists from repeating the same album.
Generic limits allow at most two recordings per artist and one per known artist/album pair. No artist or genre lookup tables. Reproduced and verified on Green Room, with before/after screenshots in the task. Three regression tests cover album repetition, unknown albums, artist limits, identical album names across artists, and exclusion of unrelated filler.

## Open concerns

MusicBrainz outages and serialized cold requests still hurt availability and latency. Career-wide artist genres can misrepresent an individual song or album. Compilation/reissue dates affect year ranking. Search can rank covers above the intended original. Filter changes temporarily replace the known seed heading with a loading heading. These are not claimed fixed. Audio relevance needs listener evaluation and stronger recording/album evidence, not more hardcoded artists.
