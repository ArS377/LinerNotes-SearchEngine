# Writing preference: For fellowship and application responses, write in first per

Writing preference: For fellowship and application responses, write in first person with concrete experience (projects, internship, technologies), explain what the experience taught me, then connect it to a specific learning goal. Keep a reflective, earnest tone with direct sentences and practical outcomes. Avoid overly polished marketing language; preserve the user’s natural phrasing and occasional longer explanatory sentences. Use specific tools/platforms when truthful, such as FastAPI, Docker, APIs, LLM SDKs, and open-source work.
